## 🛠️ How to Generate Your Video in 3 Easy Steps

1. **Voice Generation (ElevenLabs / Murf.ai):**
   - Copy the `[AI VOICEOVER TEXT]` block for each scene into ElevenLabs.
   - Choose a voice like **Adam (Deep/Engaging)** or **Rachel (Clear/Professional)**.
   - Export individual MP3 audio tracks for each slide or one continuous audio track.

2. **Avatar & Slide Video (HeyGen / InVideo / Synthesia):**
   - Upload `Slides.pptx` directly to **HeyGen** or **InVideo**.
   - Paste the script for each slide into the corresponding scene box.
   - Select an AI Tech Presenter avatar to stand in the corner of the slide deck.

3. **Auto-Captioning & Export:**
   - Turn on auto-generated bold captions in yellow/cyan text.
   - Export in 1080p HD (16:9 ratio) for your hackathon submission video!
